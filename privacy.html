Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 31, 2026

At Apex Learning KW (Pty) Ltd, we are committed to the highest standards of data protection. This policy outlines how we handle personal information in accordance with global and regional regulations.

1. Data Collection (POPIA Compliance)

We collect necessary Identity Data and Technical Data to facilitate our services in strict compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa. This includes:

  • Identity Data: Student Name, Email Address, and Age.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and usage data via our Student Portal.

2. Cross-Border Data Transfer (CITRA Compliance)

By using our services, users in the State of Kuwait acknowledge and consent to the transfer of their personal data to secure servers located in the Republic of South Africa and global cloud providers. This is necessary for:

  • Payment Processing: Handled by our PCI-DSS compliant partner, Paystack.
  • Service Delivery: Video conferencing and portal access (Zoom/Google Meet/AWS).

All transfers are conducted in compliance with CITRA/DPPR regulations to ensure student data remains protected regardless of geographical location.

3. Data Security & Financial Information

Apex Learning KW (Pty) Ltd prioritizes the security of your information. We utilize industry-standard encryption and security protocols. We do not store credit card or Knet details; all financial transactions are handled exclusively by Paystack using their secure, encrypted infrastructure.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.
