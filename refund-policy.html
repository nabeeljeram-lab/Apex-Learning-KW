Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: March 22, 2026

At Apex Learning, we strive to provide a world-class educational experience. We have designed our policy to be fair to both our students and our dedicated educators.

1. 14-Day Right of Withdrawal (Kuwait Decree Law No. 10/2026)

In accordance with Kuwaiti law, students have a 14-day right to withdraw from this contract. However, by scheduling and attending a lesson within this 14-day period, the student explicitly consents to the commencement of the service and acknowledges that the right to a refund for that specific session is waived once the lesson has been conducted[cite: 22].

2. Lesson Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • More than 24 Hours Notice: Students may reschedule or cancel a lesson at no extra cost. For cancellations, a full refund will be issued to the original payment method.
  • Less than 24 Hours Notice: Cancellations made within 24 hours of the scheduled start time are non-refundable. This ensures our tutors are compensated for their reserved time.

3. No-Show Policy

If a student does not attend a scheduled session without prior notice, the lesson will be considered "rendered" and no refund will be issued.

4. Satisfaction Guarantee

If you are unsatisfied with your first lesson, please contact us within 48 hours. We will either match you with a different tutor for a complimentary session or issue a full refund for that specific lesson.

Apex Learning is operated by Nabeel Jeram. For all refund inquiries, please contact info@apexlearningkw.com.
