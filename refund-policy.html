Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: March 31, 2026

At Apex Learning KW (Pty) Ltd, we provide premium digital assets alongside specialist-led instruction. Our "Apex Assurance" policy ensures fair treatment for both our high-achieving students and our elite academic specialists.

1. The 14-Day Right of Withdrawal

In accordance with Kuwaiti and South African consumer protection laws, you have 14 days from the date of purchase to request a refund, subject to the "Digital Access" conditions outlined in Section 2 below.

2. The IP Protection Fee (Digital Access)

Because the Apex Diagnostic Suite (including Mastery Roadmaps and proprietary Assessments) is delivered instantly upon portal login, it is classified as a non-returnable digital product. If a refund is requested after portal access but before the first live session:

  • Digital Access Fee: The Company shall retain a Non-Refundable Digital Access Fee (35% of the Bundle Price) to cover the licensed Intellectual Property already delivered.
  • Balance: The remaining balance will be refunded to the original payment method, minus a 10% administrative/banking fee.

3. The First-Session Match Guarantee

If, after the first live session, the Client feels the assigned Academic Specialist is not an ideal match, the Company will:

  • Reassign a new Specialist at no additional cost; OR
  • Issue a pro-rata refund for all remaining sessions (minus a 10% administrative fee).

4. The 24-Hour Professional Scheduling Rule

  • Cancellations/Rescheduling: Must be submitted via the Student Portal at least 24 hours in advance of the scheduled start time.
  • Late Cancellations & No-Shows: If notice is provided less than 24 hours before the session, or if the student fails to attend, the session is forfeited and deducted from the bundle balance. No refunds or make-up sessions are provided for missed time.
