Refund Policy & Privacy Protocols

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Subscription Refund Policy

Tutor listing fees cover immediate digital activation, administrative profile vetting, and platform database maintenance. Because digital listing benefits begin immediately upon activation:

  • Cancellation: You may cancel your subscription at any time. When cancelled, your active listing remains valid until the end of your current paid billing period (week or month), after which no further charges occur.
  • Renewal Charges: Renewal charges are non-refundable once processed. Tutors are encouraged to manage or pause their membership before their renewal date via the customer billing portal.
  • Duplicate or Erroneous Charges: In the event of a verified technical error or duplicate billing, full refunds are issued promptly upon contacting info@apexlearningkw.com.

2. Hourly Lesson Refunds

Hourly lesson fees are paid directly between parents and tutors. Any refund requests regarding missed sessions, schedule adjustments, or tutoring satisfaction must be handled directly between the parent and the educator.

3. Blind-Matching & Privacy Protocols

We respect the privacy of families and educators:

  • Blind Matching Integrity: Tutor profiles remain private and are not displayed on an open public classifieds board to avoid commercial bias and ensure diagnostic integrity.
  • Contact Protection: Student and guardian phone numbers and emails are never published. Details are only exchanged with a verified tutor once both the tutor and the family confirm mutual fit and agreed rates.
  • Data Usage: Information submitted through our diagnostic forms is strictly used for placement coordination and administrative contact. We do not sell or lease personal data to third-party advertisers.

4. Contact Us

Questions concerning subscriptions, refunds, or data privacy can be directed to info@apexlearningkw.com.
