Terms of Service

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Scope of Service

Apex Learning operates an independent educational concierge and diagnostic matching platform in Kuwait. We connect students and guardians with verified private tutors based on learning archetypes, curriculum requirements, and scheduling preferences.

2. Matching & Rate Brokerage

The student intake and diagnostic matching process is free for students and parents. Apex Learning acts as an introductory broker. Once a rate and schedule are mutually agreed upon by both parties, contact details are exchanged.

Lesson Payments: All instructional fees are paid directly from the family to the tutor. Apex Learning charges 0% commission on ongoing lessons and does not process hourly tutoring fees.

3. Tutor Platform Listing Subscriptions

Independent educators pay a recurring platform listing subscription (Weekly or Monthly) via Lemon Squeezy to maintain active listing status in the verified matching pool.

  • Auto-Renewal: Subscriptions automatically renew at the selected cadence (every 7 days or every 30 days) until paused or cancelled.
  • Self-Service Cancellation: Tutors may cancel or pause their subscription at any time with one click through the Lemon Squeezy customer billing portal accessible via their receipt or tutor dashboard.
  • No Placement Guarantee: While listing grants immediate eligibility for placement in matching cases, client referrals depend on incoming student demand and alignment with the tutor's subject, location, and archetype profile.

4. Independent Contractor Status

Tutors are independent educators and are not employees, partners, or agents of Apex Learning. Apex Learning is not responsible for lesson scheduling conflicts, individual instruction disputes, or direct financial transactions conducted outside of platform subscription billing.

5. Contact Information

For administrative or billing inquiries, contact us at info@apexlearningkw.com.
